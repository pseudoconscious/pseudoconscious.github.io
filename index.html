PseudoConscious Inc.

PseudoConscious Inc. is a Canadian company that supports open-source research in artificial intelligence. Our interests include large language models, AI agents, agent harnesses, and benchmarking. Our current work focuses on state-based agents and their harnesses, with taskless agents as one part of that research.

Research

ResidentBench: A proof-of-concept study of how ten language model configurations used tools in five simulated scenarios without an assigned task. Supported by PseudoConscious Inc. (preprint, code and results)

Projects

  • PseudoAgent: A state-driven harness for AI agents.
  • PseudoChat: A chat application built on PseudoAgent.
